How it works

Live in minutes. Three steps, then it runs itself.

Setup lives in your terminal — your agent does the wiring. The dashboard is just for reading the answers.

01

Your agent wires it up

Install, and let Claude Code or Cursor add one line where it matters — a diff you review.

$ npx @signal/cli init
signal.track("checkout")
02

Ask one question, in-app

At the moment that matters — right after the flow — Signal asks your user. Three seconds, then it's gone.

03

Read the score

Answers roll up into a live satisfaction score per flow, so you always know what to fix next.

Ask

The right question, at the exact right moment.

  • Fires in-context — right after checkout, right when the feature lands.
  • A native bottom sheet — not an email survey no one opens.
  • Cooldowns & sampling built in — never annoys your users.
