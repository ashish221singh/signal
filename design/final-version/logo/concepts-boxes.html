Two blocks — floating, a little misaligned

A block and its signal. Solid monochrome, off-grid on purpose — the misalignment is what makes it feel alive. In motion, the floating block drifts (and, on dark, flashes the brand orange — the palette returns in movement). Three takes:

block + signal

Echo

Two near-equal blocks, offset with a clean gap. Calm, balanced, unmistakably a pair. The smaller one floats up-right like an echo of the first.

the signal escaping

Emit

A solid core and a small tilted block breaking away — the closest to your old "tile emitting," but distilled to one floating square. Most narrative: a signal leaving the source.

off-grid pair

Twin

Two equal blocks on a diagonal, one gently rotated — maximum "floating / misaligned" tension. Feels the most contemporary and design-forward.
