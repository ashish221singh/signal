01 · Brand

Logo

The Echo mark — two rounded blocks, different sizes, deliberately misaligned: a block and its signal. Wordmark signal set in JetBrains Mono, lowercase, with an optional cursor. Static mark; life belongs to the page, not the logo.

08 · Voice

Tone

Direct, technical, quietly confident. Talks to developers like a peer. Lowercase in code contexts, sentence case in prose. Short sentences. No hype words, no exclamation marks in UI.

✓"Ship a survey in one line." · "Not eligible? It stays silent." · "Set it up from your terminal."
✕"Supercharge your feedback game!!!" · "Revolutionary AI-powered synergy." · marketing fluff.
