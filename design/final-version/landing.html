Why Signal

Feedback that actually reaches you.

Analytics tell you what users clicked. Signal tells you how they felt — flow by flow, from real users, in the moment.

Asked in context

One question, in-app, right after the flow — not a survey emailed to no one next week.

Scored per flow

Every answer rolls into a live satisfaction score for each part of your product.

Zero setup drag

Your agent creates the workflow and adds the tracking call. You just review the diff.

How it works

Live in minutes. Then it runs itself.

STEP 01

Run one command

Your agent takes it from here — creates the workflow, adds the call, opens a diff.

$ npx @signal/cli init
# agent adds:
signal.track("checkout")
STEP 02

Ask one question

At the moment that matters, a native sheet slides up. Three seconds, then it's gone.

STEP 03

Read the score

Answers become a live satisfaction score per flow — you always know what to fix next.
